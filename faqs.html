Tattoo

How much does a tattoo cost?+

Each tattoo artist has their own hourly rate, and prices depend on size and how detailed the tattoo is. Reach out if you need more on pricing.

Do you require a deposit?+

We require a $100 deposit, which comes off the price of your tattoo.

What is your cancellation policy?+

We have a 24-hour cancellation policy. If you don't cancel at least 24 hours ahead, your deposit isn't refunded.

Do you take walk-ins?+

We do take walk-ins, but appointments are preferred.

Can I bring a friend?+

Feel free to bring a friend, but keep in mind we're a small tattoo shop and space is limited.

What's the best way to contact you?+

For the quickest response, text the shop phone at (828) 606-9873. Mack, our shop manager, will get back to you as soon as possible.

Where are you, and is there parking?+

The tattoo shop is on the lower level of 15 Zillicoa St.

Park in our back lot, the small lot in front of the building, or on the street.

Hair

How much do you charge?+

Dre has two prices: new client and transformative cuts start at $175, and classic cuts for existing clients start at $150. If you're an existing client who wants a big change, pick the transformative cut.

Sam's prices depend on the color service you need; see her booking page for pricing.

How should my hair be when I arrive?+

Please come with clean, naturally dried, product-free hair.

What is your cancellation policy?+

Cancel at least 24 hours ahead. Emergencies happen; just text us.

Can I book a bang trim?+

Bang trims are for existing clients only. Book one by texting (828) 606-9873.

How late is too late?+

If you're more than 15 minutes late we'll have to reschedule, and there may be a cancellation fee. Things happen, so text the shop phone to give us a heads up.

What payment do you take?+

Card, cash or Venmo.

Do you take walk-ins?+

We don't take walk-ins for haircuts.

Where are you?+

Hair and cosmetic tattoo are on the upper level of 15 Zillicoa St: a small cottage tucked between a stone castle and an orange house.
