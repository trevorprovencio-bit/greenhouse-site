Our story

Dre, Chuck & Charlie Donoghue

“A forward-thinking, open and inclusive space where creativity and community thrive.”

Greenhouse is led by Dre and Chuck Donoghue. Dre spent more than a decade in New York, starting in the cutting program at Bumble and bumble and becoming a stylist and lead educator. Chuck tattooed at some of the city's most respected shops, including Dare Devil, Fun City, Kings Avenue and Greenpoint Tattoo Co.

In 2017 they moved to Asheville while expecting their son, Charlie, and Chuck took a position at Hot Stuff Tattoo until 2021. Starting something together had been a long-time dream. Greenhouse is that dream: hair on the top floor, tattoo on the bottom, in a little stone cottage next to Homewood.

Because of who we are, you'll find us hosting events, artists and exhibitions, from Greenhouse Academy classes to work by local artists on the walls and visiting tattooers on guest spots.

—Dre, Chuck, Charlie, and the Greenhouse family
