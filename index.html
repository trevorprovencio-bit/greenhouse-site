How booking works

Text the shopRead the FAQs
  1. 1

    Pick your artist

    Hair and tattoo artists each keep their own calendar, so you book straight with the person you want.

  2. 2

    Book online or text us

    Use the Book button on their card. Questions first, or not sure who fits? Text (828) 606-9873.

  3. 3

    Tattoos take a deposit

    A $100 deposit holds your spot and comes off the price. Cancel at least 24 hours ahead to get it back.

Walk-ins are welcome, but appointments are preferred.
