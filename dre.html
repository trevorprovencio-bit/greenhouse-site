Profile

Dre Donoghue

Hair · Asheville

“Moving to the mountains has been such a nice change for me and my family.”

Before Asheville, Dre taught cutting and styling at Bumble and bumble and worked backstage at New York fashion week. Now she does it from a little greenhouse in Montford.

“I attended and taught foundational and advanced styling and cutting classes throughout my time at Bumble. It provided a strong foundation that has allowed me to build and grow a freelance career in editorial styling, wedding styling, education, and provide cuts and styles for my in salon clients.”

In New York she worked backstage at fashion week on shows for top designers, and credits her close work under some of fashion's most influential hairstylists for her continual growth.

“I’ve taken inspiration from these experiences and love to share this approach with the hairdressers I teach.”

Today she splits her time between her chair at Greenhouse, editorial and wedding work, and teaching other hairdressers, on the road and one-on-one in Asheville.
