About Chuck

From New York’s busiest shops to a little greenhouse in Montford.

Chuck credits his years working alongside great tattooers for his growth and his diverse body of work.

In 2017, Chuck and Dre moved to Asheville while expecting their son Charlie, and Chuck was honored to take a position at Hot Stuff Tattoo until 2021. Now they're excited to share Greenhouse with the community of Asheville.
