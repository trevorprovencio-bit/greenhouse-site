Current artists

Greenhouse hosts artists and exhibitions alongside the chair and the needle. Here is who is showing now.

Current artist

Barbara Kenny

Barbara Shea Kenny is a self-taught abstract artist living in Leicester, North Carolina. Inspired by landscapes, memory and the ways people connect with the places around them, she makes colorful, layered paintings whose compositions emerge over time, made to create moments of joy and reflection.

Contact Barbara

Current artist

Susan Hendley

Susan Hendley is a ceramic artist whose practice over the past decade explores functional forms that merge color, beauty, utility and nature in hand-crafted vessels. Their work has been shown regionally and nationally, including at SOCO Gallery and Saltstone Ceramics, and they teach craft in the community.

@susanhendleystudio
